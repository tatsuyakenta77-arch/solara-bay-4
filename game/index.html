An open-world crime story in Port Meridian

Jace Marlowe is back in Port Meridian for one last job with fixer Mara Quinn. Steal cars, outrun the harbour police, settle the score at the docks — and own the city.

On foot: WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, E enter a car, F punch, click to shoot, drag mouse to look around.

Driving: W accelerate, S brake / reverse, A / D steer, Space handbrake, E step out.

Heat: stealing cars, hitting pedestrians and shooting raises your wanted level. Police chase, ram and try to bust you. Break line of sight and the heat cools off. Getting busted costs you a fine.

Finish all 6 story missions to own Port Meridian. Progress saves automatically. Esc pauses · M mutes.

An original game — no real city, person or brand is depicted. Best in desktop Chrome.
